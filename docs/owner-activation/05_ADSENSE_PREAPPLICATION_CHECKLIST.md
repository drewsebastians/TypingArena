# AdSense Pre-Application Checklist

## Repository-ready

- Stable useful typing, dictation, transcription, Career, and discovery pages.
- Clear route navigation, privacy surface, local-first explanation, and no
  fabricated competitor rows.
- `SafeAdSlot` reserves space outside tasks and removes itself during active
  typing, dictation, transcription, noise, Daily, race, team, and assessment
  work.
- No fake publisher ID, no fake `ads.txt`, and no automatic application.

## Owner/legal review before application

- Confirm site ownership and the final public origin.
- Review privacy, consent, cookie/provider wording, and any required legal
  notices for the target jurisdictions.
- Review substantive content, navigation, mobile usability, and accidental
  click risk.
- Decide whether ads remain disabled until approval and hosted verification.

## Activation procedure

After approval, add only the approved `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_CLIENT` in the
protected hosting environment, rebuild, and run the hosted smoke plus the
active-task ad-boundary matrix. Verify no ad appears inside active assessments,
races, typing, dictation, transcription, noise, or Daily tasks.

## Expected evidence

Approved publisher record, redacted environment-change record, hosted pages
with approved ad markup, active-task screenshots/video, and no accidental click
or task-interruption finding.

## Failure and rollback

If approval is pending, policy review fails, or the boundary fails, keep the
client unset. If a live placement is unsafe, remove the client key and rebuild
or roll back to the last ad-disabled artifact.

## Mutation and approval

Applying for AdSense, accepting policy terms, publishing legal changes, adding
the publisher client, or enabling live ads are external mutations requiring
owner/legal approval.
